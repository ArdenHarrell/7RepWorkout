Why I built this

I built 7RepWorkout because the way I used to work out kept failing me.

Too heavy, and I got hurt. I lifted too heavy or did too many sets because I wasn't keeping track. With 7Rep you start at 70% of your max (), use one weight per exercise, and track it on your sheet.

No hours for the gym. My life was busy. I needed every session under an hour. With 7Rep you can work out and get on with your day.

Muscles I ignored. I always skipped the ones I liked less, and they never grew. With 7Rep, every week trains all 13 muscle groups.

Sporadic workouts, sporadic results. I'd stick with a plan for two weeks, then miss a week or two because of double shifts, and I'd be back at ground zero. With 7Rep, if you miss a week or two, you just keep going where you left off.

How to find your max

  1. Warm up with a light set.
  2. Pick a weight for the exercise and try 15 reps.
  3. Can't finish 15? Drop 5 lb and try again. Keep going until you can.
  4. The heaviest weight you can lift for 15 reps is your max.
  5. Start at 70% of that weight, rounded to the nearest weight you have.

Repeat for each exercise, since each one gets its own weight.
